About the Workshop

The Hands-on Wildlife Genomics Workshop is an intensive five-day training designed for early-career researchers, conservation practitioners, and university students interested in applying genomic approaches to wildlife research and biodiversity conservation. Participants will gain practical experience in laboratory methods, bioinformatics analyses, and real-world conservation case studies.

Workshop Highlights

Population Genomics

Variant calling, population structure, genetic diversity and conservation genomics.

eDNA

Environmental DNA workflows for biodiversity monitoring and ecosystem assessment.

Metagenomics

Sequencing microbial communities using modern genomic techniques.

Bioinformatics

Linux, R, quality control, variant analysis, and reproducible workflows.

Training Tracks
