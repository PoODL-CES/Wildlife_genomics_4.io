About the Workshop

The Hands-on Wildlife Genomics Workshop is a five-day immersive training bringing together early-career researchers, university students, and conservation practitioners interested in applying genomic approaches to wildlife conservation. Participants gain hands-on experience in laboratory protocols, DNA extraction, library preparation, population genomics, environmental DNA, metagenomics and bioinformatics using real conservation datasets.

Workshop Tracks

Population Genomics

Explore variant discovery, population structure, genetic diversity, phylogenetics and conservation genomics.

Environmental DNA and Metagenomics

Learn environmental sampling, DNA extraction, library preparation and biodiversity monitoring. Investigate microbial communities using modern sequencing technologies.

Lab methods in Wildlife Genomics

Practical laboratory sessions covering DNA extraction and molecular techniques for biodiversity and conservation genomics.

📍 Venue

The 4th Hands-on Wildlife Genomics Workshop will be held at Pwani University Bioscience Research Centre (PUBReC), Pwani University, Kilifi County, Kenya.


PUBReC is a multidisciplinary research centre supporting research in wildlife genomics, biodiversity conservation, marine science, microbiology, environmental DNA (eDNA), and bioinformatics.


📅 Dates: 10–14 August 2026 & 19-21 August 2026
